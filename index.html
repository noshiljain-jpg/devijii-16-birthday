Dear Devijii 🤍

Dear Devijii,

Today marks another year of your existence on this beautiful planet, and honestly? I'm really glad you decided to show up on October 19, 2010. 😭

It's funny because our friendship is still so new—just about to hit 3 months in October— but somehow you've already managed to become one of my favorite people. Like, how does that even work? How do you go from being someone I just met to someone I can't imagine not having around?

And let me be honest, you're quite the character. 😂
You get angry at me. Like, genuinely angry. The kind of anger where I'm standing there wondering what I did wrong this time.
You give me orders. Not ask, not suggest—just straight up command me to do things. And somehow, I end up doing them.
Your mood swings are legendary. Literally. One minute you're laughing, the next minute I'm worried I said something wrong.
And your memory? Don't even get me started. You'll forget half the important things I tell you, but somehow remember that one tiny detail I mentioned in passing. 🤣

But here's the thing, Devijii…
Despite all of that—or maybe because of all of that—you're genuinely special to me.
Your anger shows me you care. Your orders show me your confidence. Your mood swings show me you're real, not fake. And your forgetfulness? Well, it's just another thing that makes you uniquely, wonderfully you.

As you turn 16 today, I hope you know how precious you are.
I hope you chase your dreams fearlessly.
I hope you keep that beautiful smile even when things get tough.
I hope you know that you deserve all the happiness, all the good moments, and all the good people coming into your life.
And I hope that I get to be a part of many more of your birthdays and memories.

Happy Birthday to my very own Devijii. 🤍☕
Here's to 16 and everything waiting for you ahead.

— Your Bestie 🤍
